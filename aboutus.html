Who are we?

Tenchi Construction is a multidisciplinary construction and architectural drafting company specializing in 2D working drawings, 3D interior modeling, structural renovations, and turnkey building. Founded on the principles of accuracy, craftsmanship, and spatial innovation, we serve homeowners, commercial clients, and industry professionals seeking reliable structural solutions.
