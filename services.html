Services

Comprehensive Architectural & Building Solutions

From technical drafting board to the construction site.

Tenchi Construction provides high-end 3D architectural visualization and photorealistic rendering, enabling clients to clearly visualize spatial layouts, natural lighting, and interior finishes before construction begins. We specialize in producing fully compliant municipal submission drawings and detailed structural CAD packages tailored for both residential and commercial developments. Our expertise extends to structural alterations, including open-plan kitchen remodels, custom floating staircases, and full turnkey building solutions. From foundational earthworks to intricate concrete structural detailing, our team ensures every project adheres strictly to engineering standards and safety codes. By seamlessly integrating precise design plans with hands-on construction management, we deliver seamless renovations and new builds tailored to each client's vision.
